Good to Know

Studio Policies & FAQ

A valid, government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license, or national ID) is required for every client. Guests under 18 must be accompanied by a legal guardian with valid ID for eligible piercings; some tattoo services are strictly 18+.

A 50% deposit is required before an appointment is confirmed and is deducted from your final session cost. Your booking remains pending until the studio verifies payment. Rescheduling requires at least 48 hours' notice; cancellations within 48 hours forfeit the deposit.

Our artists bring fully sterilized, single-use equipment and portable setups to your home. It's faster to book, more comfortable, and ideal for private sessions. Select "Home Visit" in the booking form — a small travel fee may apply depending on distance.

Keep the bandage on for 2-4 hours, then gently wash with fragrance-free soap. Apply a thin layer of recommended aftercare balm 2-3 times daily, avoid direct sunlight, swimming, and tight clothing for at least 2 weeks. Never pick or scratch peeling skin.

Clean twice daily with sterile saline solution, avoid touching with unwashed hands, and do not rotate jewelry unnecessarily. Healing time varies from 4 weeks (earlobes) to 12 months (cartilage/navel). Contact us immediately if you notice excessive redness or discharge.
