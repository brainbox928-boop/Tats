Good to know, honestly

Why crypto, and is it safe?

Crypto lets you pay quickly from anywhere without sharing bank or card details with us. The one thing to know is that blockchain transfers can't be reversed by a bank, which is why we ask you to double-check the address and network before sending, and why we verify every payment by hand.

What if I sent it on the wrong network or to a wrong address?

Contact us right away at +1 681 505 6975 with your order reference and TXID. We'll help where we can, but funds sent to an address that isn't ours can't be recovered by us, so please verify first.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Rescheduling needs at least 48 hours' notice. Cancellations within 48 hours forfeit the deposit. See the studio policies on our FAQ for the full details.

How do I know you received my payment?

You'll see a confirmation page straight after submitting, and we'll message you personally once the transfer is verified. If you haven't heard from us, call or email us with your order reference.
